I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science (DCC) at the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG), Brazil. I work in the <a href="https://www.verlab.dcc.ufmg.br/">Computer Vision and Robotics Laboratory (VeRLab)</a> and take part in GEAR, the department's advanced robotics study group. I am also a co-founder of <a href="https://mirarobotica.com">MIRA</a>, a Brazilian company that builds LiDAR survey tools, 3D mapping systems and inspection robots for caves, ducts and dam galleries.

My research is about robots that go where people should not: autonomous exploration, mapping and navigation in confined, subterranean and industrial environments, with ground, aerial and multi-robot systems.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from UFMG in 2022. My thesis on terrain-aware autonomous exploration won the CAPES Thesis Award for the best PhD thesis in Computer Science in Brazil (2023). Before joining the faculty in 2024, I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Meta, a computer scientist at Komatsu, and a researcher at the Instituto Tecnológico Vale (ITV), where I coordinated work on the EspeleoRobô, a robot for inspecting caves and confined spaces.
