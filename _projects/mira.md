[MIRA](https://mirarobotica.com) designs and builds, in Brazil, the hardware and software to map spaces that people should not enter. The work started at UFMG and grew through research projects with Vale, the Instituto Tecnológico Vale (ITV) and Petrobras, including the [EspeleoRobô]({{ '/research/espeleorobo/' | relative_url }}) cave-inspection robot.

MIRA's products cover three levels of survey:

- **2D survey tool**: measures distance, inclination and azimuth at each station, so the crew draws the floor plan during the survey.
- **3D localization and mapping**: a LiDAR scanner captures the space, and MIRA Cloud segments the point cloud and measures each cross-section (area, width, height, floor roughness) in the browser.
- **Robotic inspection**: robots go in when entering is a fatal risk.

Typical applications are cave surveys for mining permits, geological mapping of dam galleries, inspection of drains and ducts, and confined spaces in oil and gas.

I co-founded MIRA with Gilmar Cruz Jr (CEO), Gustavo Freitas and Paulo Rezeck.
