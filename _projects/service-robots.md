This line of projects develops a low-cost mapping and navigation system that can be mounted on different service robots, including ground vehicles, drones and robot arms, in production plants. It combines sensor fusion for localization, point-cloud mapping with LiDAR SLAM, and planning and control modules (graph and sampling-based planners, vector fields and predictive control), with implementations aimed at embedded hardware.

Recent results include an embeddable mapping and navigation unit, LiDAR–robot and LiDAR–camera calibration for multi-sensor fusion, instrumented wheels with nanostructured sensors for weight sensing and terrain classification, and [EKF-LOAM](https://doi.org/10.1109/tase.2022.3169442), which fuses LiDAR SLAM with wheel odometry and inertial data for spaces with few geometric features.

**Funding:** CNPq (Technological Development 2020 and 2023, Universal 2021) and FAPEMIG (Universal 2022). Coordinated by Gustavo Freitas.
