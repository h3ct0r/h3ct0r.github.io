At ITV I worked on several projects with Vale that use robots and machine learning to make mining operations safer. I coordinated a framework for monitoring conveyor belts (2016–2021) that used mobile robots and drones for visual inspection, together with augmented-reality tools for the inspectors. Other projects included the ROSI robot for harsh outdoor inspection, automatic preparation of steel-cord belt splices, deep-learning detection of damage in tailing pipes, hyperspectral detection of contaminated soil, an autonomous platform for loading explosives, and, at UFMG, teleoperation of mining equipment with haptic and immersive interfaces.

Collaborations in this area continue, for example on estimating iron-ore particle size during crushing and on auditable safety inspections with robots and blockchain.
