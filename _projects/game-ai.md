Real-time strategy games such as StarCraft are a hard testbed for AI: many units, partial information and decisions in real time. With colleagues at UFMG we worked on evolving swarm intelligence for task allocation (Best Paper Award, SBGames 2014) and on game-theoretic strategy selection (AIIDE 2016). Our bots placed 3rd in the IEEE CIG 2016 student StarCraft competition and in the top 10 of the AIIDE 2016 competition, and our team took 2nd place in the Rescue Agent Simulation league of the Latin American RoboCup 2015.
