Teams of robots can cover more ground and keep a link to the operator, but only if they coordinate their motion and their communication. This work covers both ends of the problem:

- **Swarm platforms and behaviors.** [HeRo](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10514-023-10100-0) is an open, low-cost robot for swarm robotics research and teaching. Current work studies how a swarm can switch between behaviors to avoid congestion in cluttered environments.
- **Communication-aware planning.** Path planning and multi-robot navigation that keep radio links or line of sight between robots during assisted inspections, including a PhD project on navigation guided by signal quality.
