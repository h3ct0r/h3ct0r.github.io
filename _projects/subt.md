Team CoSTAR, led by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, competed in the DARPA Subterranean Challenge with NeBula, an autonomy architecture for exploring caves, tunnels and urban underground spaces with teams of ground and aerial robots. I am a co-author of the addendum that extends NeBula to larger-scale environments, published in IEEE Transactions on Field Robotics (2024).
