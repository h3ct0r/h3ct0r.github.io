Small drones are fast to deploy but have little onboard computing and short flight times. Current projects look at search and rescue, with person detection processed off board over long-range Wi-Fi HaLow links and with RGB cameras, and at reinforcement-learning navigation for exploration drones.

Earlier work includes an autonomous delivery drone, thermographic inspection of conveyor belts from UAVs, collaborative teleoperation of drones, [aerial magnetic surveys]({{ '/research/magnetic-surveys/' | relative_url }}) and [autonomous drone racing]({{ '/research/drone-racing/' | relative_url }}). From 2019 to 2024 this also included active sensing of regions with multiple UAVs, funded by FAPEMIG.
