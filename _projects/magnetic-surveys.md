Aeromagnetic surveys are usually flown by crewed aircraft. In my MSc and early research at UFMG and ITV we showed that small autonomous drones carrying a fluxgate magnetometer can do the job, and developed multi-robot coverage path planning based on hexagonal segmentation to split the survey area among several drones. The work ended with cooperative digital magnetic-elevation maps built by teams of small aerial robots, published in the *Journal of Field Robotics* (2019).
