XQuad was a team of UFMG graduate students and professors created to compete in AlphaPilot and in the Artificial Intelligence Robotic Racing (AIRR) league run by the Drone Racing League with Lockheed Martin. The team qualified for the 2019 AIRR season and finished 7th. We described our integrated solution for autonomous racing in indoor environments in *Intelligent Service Robotics* (2021).
